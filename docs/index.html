Plan beyond this week

Any date. Any time.
Even a year ahead.

A regular alarm only looks a week ahead. Task in the Mind rings on the exact date you pick: next Tuesday, a friend's birthday, or a bill due next September.

Rings, not pings

Your chosen tone keeps ringing until you tap Got it. Hard to ignore, impossible to miss.

Reschedule in a tap

Push it 5, 10 or 15 minutes, or drag the clock hands to a new date and time.

Lists that stay tidy

Work, Gym, Groceries: swipe between lists and hold a tab to reorder.

Safe in your Drive

Optional backup to a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive. New phone? Restore in a tap.

Always up to date

The app tells you when a new version is out. Update in a tap.
