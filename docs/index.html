Task in the Mind

Tasks that ring like a real alarm, so nothing slips your mind.

Download for Android

Free · No ads · No account needed · Android 8+

Plan beyond this week

Any date. Any time.
Even a year ahead.

A regular alarm only looks a week ahead. Task in the Mind rings on the exact date you pick: next Tuesday, a friend's birthday, or a bill due next September.

Everything it does

Small app, big memory

Rings, not pings

  • Your tone loops until you act
  • Full-screen on the lock screen
  • Strong, steady vibration

Reschedule in a tap

  • 5, 10 or 15 minutes later
  • Or drag the clock hands
  • Pick a new date too

Lists that stay tidy

  • Work, Gym, Groceries, you name it
  • Swipe between lists
  • Hold a tab to reorder

Safe in your Drive

  • Private, hidden backup folder
  • Syncs after every change
  • New phone? Restore in a tap

Always up to date

  • The app tells you when a new version is out, and updates in a tap. Your tasks stay put.
